Breakfast Recipes

Scrambled Eggs with Avocado and Cheese

Ingredients:

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp butter or coconut oil
  • 1/4 avocado (sliced)
  • 1 oz cheddar cheese (shredded)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Melt butter or coconut oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  2. Whisk eggs in a bowl. Pour into the hot pan.
  3. Cook, stirring occasionally with a spatula, until the eggs are almost set.
  4. Sprinkle the shredded cheddar cheese over the eggs and continue to cook until the cheese is melted and eggs are cooked to your liking.
  5. Serve immediately, topped with fresh avocado slices.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

Keto Smoothie

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1/2 cup fresh spinach
  • 1/4 avocado
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 scoop low-carb protein powder (vanilla or unflavored recommended)
  • A few berries (e.g., 3-4 raspberries or blueberries, optional for slight sweetness)
  • Ice cubes (optional, for a thicker smoothie)

Instructions:

  1. Combine all ingredients (almond milk, spinach, avocado, chia seeds, protein powder, and berries if using) in a blender.
  2. Add ice cubes if desired for a thicker consistency.
  3. Blend on high speed until all ingredients are thoroughly combined and the smoothie is smooth.
  4. Pour into a glass and serve immediately.

Lunch Recipes

Grilled Chicken Salad

Ingredients:

  • 4 oz grilled chicken breast (cooked and sliced or diced)
  • 2 cups mixed leafy greens (e.g., romaine, spinach, arugula)
  • 1/2 cucumber (sliced)
  • 1/4 bell pepper (any color, sliced)
  • 1/4 cup cherry tomatoes (halved, optional)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil (extra virgin)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice (freshly squeezed)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, combine the mixed leafy greens, sliced cucumber, sliced bell pepper, and cherry tomatoes (if using).
  2. Top the salad with the grilled chicken slices.
  3. In a small bowl or jar, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper to create the dressing.
  4. Drizzle the dressing over the salad just before serving.
  5. Toss gently to combine all ingredients.

Tuna Salad Lettuce Wraps

Ingredients:

  • 1 can (5 oz) tuna in olive oil or water, drained well
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise (full-fat, check for no added sugar)
  • 1 celery stalk (finely chopped)
  • 1 tbsp chopped red onion (optional)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • 4-6 large lettuce leaves (e.g., romaine, iceberg, or butter lettuce)

Instructions:

  1. In a medium bowl, combine the drained tuna, mayonnaise, chopped celery, and chopped red onion (if using).
  2. Mix well with a fork until all ingredients are evenly combined.
  3. Season with salt and pepper to your taste.
  4. Carefully wash and dry the lettuce leaves.
  5. Spoon the tuna salad mixture evenly into the center of each lettuce leaf.
  6. Serve immediately as wraps.

Dinner Recipes

Baked Salmon with Asparagus

Ingredients:

  • 4 oz salmon fillet (skin-on or skinless)
  • 1 cup asparagus spears (trimmed)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon wedge
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Toss the asparagus spears with half of the olive oil, a pinch of salt, and pepper directly on one side of the baking sheet.
  3. Pat the salmon fillet dry. Drizzle with the remaining olive oil. Season generously with garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  4. Place the seasoned salmon fillet on the other side of the baking sheet next to the asparagus.
  5. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through (flakes easily with a fork) and the asparagus is tender-crisp. Cooking time may vary depending on the thickness of the salmon.
  6. Serve immediately with a fresh lemon wedge to squeeze over the salmon.

Cauliflower Mash with Ground Beef

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup cauliflower florets (about 1/4 medium head)
  • 4 oz ground beef (80/20 recommended for flavor)
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese (or your favorite cheese)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or avocado oil (for cooking beef)
  • Optional: 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Steam the cauliflower florets until very tender (about 10-15 minutes). You can also boil them.
  2. While cauliflower is steaming, heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and cook, breaking it apart with a spoon, until browned. Drain any excess fat.
  3. Stir the garlic powder, salt, and pepper into the cooked ground beef. Reduce heat to low and keep warm.
  4. Once the cauliflower is tender, drain it well. Add the steamed cauliflower, butter, and shredded cheese to a bowl or food processor. Mash with a potato masher or process until smooth (or your desired consistency). Season the mash with salt and pepper.
  5. To serve, spoon the cauliflower mash onto a plate and top with the seasoned ground beef.
  6. Garnish with fresh parsley if desired.

Celery Sticks with Cream Cheese

Ingredients:

  • 2-3 medium celery stalks
  • 2 tbsp full-fat cream cheese
  • Optional: A sprinkle of everything bagel seasoning or black pepper

Instructions:

  1. Wash the celery stalks thoroughly and trim the ends.
  2. Cut the celery stalks into desired lengths (e.g., 3-4 inch sticks).
  3. Spread the cream cheese into the groove of each celery stick.
  4. If desired, sprinkle with everything bagel seasoning or black pepper for extra flavor.
  5. Serve immediately or store in the refrigerator for a quick snack.
